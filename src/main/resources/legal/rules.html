Game Rules

Code of Conduct · incorporated into the Terms of Service (§5)

These rules apply to everyone — across the game and our Discord. By playing Oasis or taking part in the community, you agree to uphold them at all times. They exist to protect the experience for everyone, not to restrict individuals.

Staff have the final say on all decisions. If you believe an action taken against you was unfair, open a support ticket rather than arguing publicly. Violations are assessed case by case — severity, intent, and prior history are all taken into account.

  2. Gameplay

◆ Community

1 · Respect & Conduct

  • 1.a Harassment, hate speech, racism, and targeted bullying are not tolerated — in-game or on Discord.
  • 1.b Flaming and flame-baiting are not welcome — you are responsible for your responses regardless of provocation.
  • 1.c Censor evasion and political or religious discussion in public channels are not permitted.

⚠ Escalation — Warning → mute → suspension. Severe or targeted cases → immediate permanent ban.

2 · Disruptive Behaviour

  • 2.a Griefing, blocking progress, or sabotaging cooperative content is prohibited.
  • 2.b Spamming, chat flooding, or excessive pinging across any channel is not tolerated.
  • 2.c Yell is a privilege — no URLs, extreme language, controversy, server-bashing, or public disrespect toward Oasis.

⚠ Escalation — Warning → mute → temporary ban for repeat or severe cases.

3 · Identity & Impersonation

  • 3.a Offensive, discriminatory, or impersonating account names will be forcibly changed or removed.
  • 3.b Impersonating staff, moderators, or other players — in any form — is strictly forbidden.
  • 3.c Accounts created with intent to mislead or confuse others about your identity are prohibited.

⚠ Escalation — Forced rename for minor violations. Impersonating staff → immediate ban.

4 · Advertising & Spam

  • 4.a Advertising other servers, games, or services is not permitted in-game or on Discord.
  • 4.b Wearing a competing server’s clan tag in-game is indirect advertising — minimum one-week timeout.
  • 4.c Unsolicited promotion via DMs to community members is also covered under this rule.

⚠ Escalation — Warning → temporary ban. Deliberate or mass advertising → immediate permanent ban.

◆ Gameplay

5 · Cheating & Automation

  • 5.a Bots, macros, AHK scripts, or any tool that automates in-game actions are forbidden — AFK or not.
  • 5.b Covers auto-clickers, mouse recorders, auto-switchers, auto-alchers, and prayer-flick tools.
  • 5.c Modified clients or code injections providing any unfair advantage are also prohibited.

⚠ Escalation — Temporary ban for first offence. Permanent ban for repeat or severe cases. No exceptions.

6 · Bug Abuse & Exploitation

  • 6.a Exploiting bugs, glitches, or unintended mechanics for personal gain is prohibited — even temporarily.
  • 6.b Report bugs immediately by opening a support ticket. Do not repeat or share details with non-staff until resolved.
  • 6.c Punishment scales with the nature, duration, and scale of the abuse.

⚠ Escalation — Stat reset / item removal → temporary ban → permanent ban for deliberate large-scale abuse.

7 · Multi-Logging & PVM Farming

  • 7.a Playing on multiple accounts across different worlds is permitted.
  • 7.b Using multiple accounts to farm the same monster spawns across worlds simultaneously is not.
  • 7.c This covers Cave Horrors, Temple Spiders, outside GWD boss rooms, and similar areas.

⚠ Escalation — Verbal warning → item removal → temporary ban, scaled to the extent and intent of the farming.

◆ Economy & Trading

8 · Scamming & Fraud

  • 8.a Agreed deals must be honoured — last-second swaps or backing out after confirmation is scamming.
  • 8.b Price manipulation — artificially inflating or deflating item values for personal gain — is prohibited.
  • 8.c Report scams by opening a support ticket. Item recovery is not guaranteed, but offenders will always be actioned.

⚠ Escalation — Warning → temp ban → permanent ban for serial offenders.

9 · Real-World Trading (RWT)

  • 9.a Oasis GP and items may only be traded for Oasis GP or items — no external currency or goods.
  • 9.b Buying, selling, or transferring accounts, items, or GP for real money is prohibited.
  • 9.c Account training for compensation, and even suggesting an RWT deal, are grounds for a ban.

⚠ Escalation — Immediate permanent ban. All items and GP are removed from the game. No exceptions.

10 · Gambling

  • 10.a Player-hosted gambling of any kind is prohibited regardless of consent — all parties are equally liable.
  • 10.b The only sanctioned method is Flower Poker (Challenge) in the official gambling area north of Home.
  • 10.c The area being unavailable does not permit player-run games of chance.

⚠ Escalation — Items removed. All parties receive a mute or ban, escalating with repeat offences.

11 · Voting Integrity

  • 11.a Do not bypass or manipulate the voting cooldown on any top-list or vote page.
  • 11.b VPN voting, multi-account stacking, or any method to inflate vote counts is prohibited.
  • 11.c Limit: up to 3 site votes per account — no more than 3 combined votes across accounts within any 12-hour window.

⚠ Escalation — Permanent ban. Severe abuse may result in Oasis being removed from affected vote sites.

◆ Security & Accounts

12 · Privacy & Personal Safety

  • 12.a Sharing personal player information without consent (doxxing) — names, IPs, addresses — is prohibited.
  • 12.b Threats toward players or the server, including DDoS threats, will not be tolerated under any circumstances.
  • 12.c Attempts to compromise the server or any player’s account are treated at maximum severity.

⚠ Escalation — Immediate permanent ban. Serious incidents may be reported to the relevant authorities.

13 · Ban Evasion & Account Responsibility

  • 13.a All activity on your account is your responsibility — “someone else did it” is not a valid defence.
  • 13.b Using a second account to bypass any active ban, mute, or restriction is prohibited.
  • 13.c Sharing credentials does not reduce accountability — if it’s on your account, it’s on you.

⚠ Escalation — Evasion accounts → immediate permanent ban. The original punishment is extended.

14 · Chargebacks

  • 14.a Initiating a chargeback on any Oasis purchase is a direct violation of our Terms.
  • 14.b Chargebacks undermine our financial stability and are taken very seriously — zero exceptions.
  • 14.c For billing concerns, contact staff via a ticket before taking external action — we will work with you.

⚠ Escalation — Immediate permanent ban on all associated accounts. No exceptions. No appeals.

◆ Punishments & Appeals

Every case is assessed at staff discretion based on three factors:

SeverityHow serious the rule breach was.
IntentWhether it was deliberate or accidental.
HistoryWhether you have prior offences on record.

Minor offences typically escalate: warning → mute → temporary ban. Severe offences — including RWT, doxxing, cheating, and chargebacks — result in immediate permanent action with no prior warning required.

15 · Appealing a Decision

  • 15.a Open a support ticket to begin an appeal.
  • 15.b Remain calm and provide any relevant context or evidence.
  • 15.c Do not publicly argue, complain, or campaign against a decision in server channels.
Staff decisions are final. Appealing in bad faith or harassing staff during the process will result in the punishment being extended.
